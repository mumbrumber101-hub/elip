          How to use this logic map

          • Click a node to read its meaning; the map will highlight its immediate causes + consequences.
          • Shift+Click a node to also center the diagram on it.
          • Scroll to zoom and drag empty space to pan.
          • Use Search for IDs (A1, S5…) or concepts (“Stoicheio”, “Pavilion”).
          • Prev/Next steps through the narrative state-machine S0→S8.
          • B toggles a bookmark for the selected node; notes + bookmarks autosave locally.
          • Filters lets you hide/show whole categories to reduce visual load.

          Everything stays self-contained: no external libraries, no servers. Your notes are stored in your browser’s local storage.
