## Register rules
- Sentence-length may be broken where Latin periods exceed readable English, but never across an argumentative joint (antecedent/consequent, licet/nihilominus).
- Connective particles (nam, etenim, quare, propterea, quandoquidem) always rendered; they carry the argument's skeleton.
- No modernizing paraphrase of scholastic machinery; the reader is presumed to want the machinery.
